# V52-C02.1 — Knowledge Lifecycle: Bounded Scope & Dependent Candidates

**Status:** `documentation` — records the bounded scope of C02 and the items deferred to dependent candidates.

**Purpose:** Serve as the canonical reference for which backlog candidates depend on C02 and when those candidates should revisit C02 artifacts. This is not an implementation task; it documents the integration surface.

---

## 1. Bounded Scope Statement

> **C02 is "implemented" for the bounded scope of:** a project-scoped knowledge capture & lifecycle system with manual candidate proposal, user-approved staging + promotion (with UI confirmation), checkpoint handoff, scoped retrieval via `readKnowledgeRecords`, and curated MOC/INDEX navigation — all validated in a delivery workspace (exp4) with ~20 records. Items marked "deferred" below are **active topics in dependent candidates** (C03, C04, C05, C08) and will be refined/integrated as those candidates are implemented. C02 closure does not block any of these; it simply defines the contract surface that dependent candidates will extend.

| 2 | **Secret/credential detection at persistence boundary** | No secret detector; C02 mentions "must be considered at persistence boundaries" but defers implementation. | **C04** (governance/safety) — primary | When C04 defines deterministic safety controls and secret-detection boundary (C04 §V5 direction: "secret detector must avoid keyword-only matching and be tested with safe synthetic positives"). |
